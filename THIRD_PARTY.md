# Third-party components

- **UZDoom 5.0.0-rc.3** is GPLv3+. The release bundle includes UZDoom's own
  `licenses.zip`. The customized renderer is reproducible from upstream commit
  `4ca590945524330d94530c0558c8d547d457e16c` plus
  [`renderer/uzdoom-ortho-renderer.patch`](renderer/uzdoom-ortho-renderer.patch).
- Camera and aiming work was adapted from Dileep V. Reddy's CC0
  `gzdoom_isometric_demo` and Jay0's Aim Assist Mod v0.8.
- **Voxel Doom II** is an optional, separately downloaded add-on by Cheello and
  the Voxel Doom Team. It is not included in this repository or its releases.
- Commercial Doom IWADs are not included. Players must supply legally owned
  `DOOM2.WAD` or `DOOM.WAD` game data.
